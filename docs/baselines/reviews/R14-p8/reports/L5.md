## 5. Findings

### Critical

**C1 — No feature can satisfy the KEEP gate; all decisions are provisional.**
The R14 KEEP rule requires n>=5
(`docs/baselines/reviews/R14-p8/README.md:16`). The only utility battery has
n=2 per cell (`evidence/utility-limitations.md:5-8`; `metrics-p8.jsonl`), and
after exclusions two cells are n=1. Therefore no F1-F11 feature can be KEEP on
utility evidence, and the 4/5 REMOVE gate cannot be balanced by KEEP votes.
This is a rule-evidence mismatch, not a code defect. The owner must either
re-run at n>=5 or amend the pre-registered rule before accepting any final
REMOVE/KEEP set.

**C2 — The only utility battery predates the reviewed baseline.**
`manifest.json` pins `kg_revision` `6dbe57a`; HEAD is `e74deab`, 22 commits
later, including the R14 remediation and the post-report fix cycle
(`impact` universe, `tests` fast path). The battery therefore measures neither
`5706dff` nor `e74deab`. Any utility number used for KEEP/REMOVE must be
re-measured at the reviewed binary; otherwise the verdict is about a
different build.

### Important

**I1 — `kg api` has negative utility on all three valid hosts.**
A1 control -> treatment: grok 3.5 -> 4.0 (+14%), agy 2.0 -> 4.5 (+125%),
zcode 3.5 -> 4.5 (+29%); tokens +12%, +27%, -16%; correctness parity 6/6.
Yet both skills route the question (`skills/kg-first/SKILL.md:26`, external
`code-index/SKILL.md` line 33). The claim exceeds the evidence; DEMOTE.

**I2 — F11 has no measured consumer.**
`--exclude-generated` is used 0 times in 2,913 journal rows on six surfaces
(`src/cli.rs:134-136`, `183-185`, `197-199`, `244-246`, `261-263`, `270-272`).
`kg files` 60 rows and `kg stats` 53 rows show the tag passively. There is no
P8 utility task and no real-corpus GT. The filter surface is unpriced; remove
it or demote it, but do not claim value.

**I3 — F2's `[ambiguous]` branch in impact is unreachable.**
`src/query.rs:1482-1490` joins `symbols ds ON ds.id = e.dst_id` and
`src/query.rs:1503-1507` tags `conf == "AMBIGUOUS"`. AMBIGUOUS edges have
NULL `dst_id` (`schema.sql:59` nullable; comment `src/query.rs:692-693`), so
the inner join drops those rows and the tag cannot fire. The `--context` help
and any `[ambiguous]` expectation in impact detail overstate the contract.
This strengthens the F2 removal vote; L1/L4 may also flag it.

**I4 — `utility-limitations.md` overstates post-exclusion n.**
Line 11 says "58/60 valid-host cells, still n=2 per cell." The two excluded
agy control cells are `agy-kgc-r1-I1` and `agy-kgc-r1-H1`
(`metrics-p8.jsonl`, violations), leaving those cells at n=1. The I1 agy
control mean 18.0 and H1 agy control mean 18.0 are single runs, not n=2.
Directional conclusions for I1/H1 at agy are weaker than the limitations
document states.

**I5 — The journal cannot support demand inference.**
73.5% of rows fall in minutes with >=20 calls; 97.8% of result rows have an
empty session; one workspace; 83 MCP result rows. Distinct shapes collapse
(imports 499 rows -> 14 shapes; tests 72 -> 9; complexity 76 -> 4). Zero-use
findings for F6 filters and F11 are exposure facts, not proof of no demand.
This caveat applies to every "Demand" cell in section 2.

**I6 — F6 resolve demand is probe-heavy, and the filters are unused.**
